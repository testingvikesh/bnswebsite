Framework

BNS – SWOC™ Business Navachar Framework

Solutions · Wealth · Opportunities · Critics — a practical BNS lens for seeing business differently.

Letter Meaning BNS Definition Business Example
S Solutions Business is not about selling products. Business is about solving problems. The bigger the problem you solve, the bigger your business becomes. Uber solved taxi finding. Zomato solved food delivery. Google solved finding information.
W Wealth Everything around you is Wealth. People, knowledge, time, technology, relationships, nature, AI and even challenges are forms of wealth. Rain creates farming wealth. No rain creates opportunities in drip irrigation, water harvesting and solar businesses.
O Opportunities Every challenge hides an opportunity. Successful entrepreneurs see opportunities where others only see problems. COVID created opportunities for Zoom, online education, home delivery, telemedicine and digital payments. AI is creating new business opportunities today.
C Critics Critics are Future Customers. Listen to complaints, improve your product or service, and convert critics into loyal customers. Customer complaints led Amazon to improve returns and support. Today, customer trust is one of its biggest strengths.
