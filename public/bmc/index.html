    About This Partnership

    • Business Navachar School (BNS) is India's pioneering weekly business education initiative dedicated to nurturing the next generation of entrepreneurs, innovators, and leaders.
    • Through this proposed partnership with the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC), we aim to introduce a structured Junior Business School Program for students from Std. 6 to Std. 9, enabling them to develop practical business knowledge, financial literacy, leadership, communication, innovation, and AI-ready skills alongside their regular academic education.
    • Our vision is to transform BMC schools into centers of entrepreneurial excellence and prepare students not only for jobs, but to become future job creators and nation builders.

    Problem Statement

    Today's education system builds Academic Knowledge, but students also need Practical Skills, Financial Awareness, and an Entrepreneurial Mindset to succeed in the rapidly changing world. Business education bridges this gap by preparing students for the future economy.

    Current Education Gap

    • Students gain Academic Knowledge, but have limited exposure to Real-World Business Skills.
    • Financial Literacy is rarely taught as a structured life skill.
    • Students have limited opportunities to develop Entrepreneurial Thinking.
    • Traditional education focuses more on Examinations than Practical Learning.
    • Skills such as Leadership, Communication, and Problem Solving need greater emphasis.
    • There is a significant gap between Classroom Learning and Industry Expectations.

    AI Era Challenges

    • Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the future of work.
    • Many traditional jobs are evolving due to Automation and Digital Technologies.
    • Future careers will require Creativity, Innovation, and Critical Thinking.
    • Students must develop AI-Ready Skills alongside academic education.
    • The ability to Learn, Adapt, and Innovate will become more valuable than routine tasks.
    • Schools must prepare students for a rapidly changing Global Economy.

    Employment Challenges

    • India's growing youth population requires more Employment Opportunities.
    • The country needs more Job Creators, not only Job Seekers.
    • Early exposure to Entrepreneurship encourages students to create opportunities for themselves and others.
    • Students should understand Business, Innovation, and Value Creation from an early age.
    • Developing an Entrepreneurial Mindset can contribute to long-term economic growth.
    • Business education supports the vision of Startup India, Skill India, and Viksit Bharat 2047.

    Financial Literacy Gap

    • Many students complete their education without learning Money Management.
    • Basic concepts such as Saving, Budgeting, Investing, and Financial Planning are essential life skills.
    • Early Financial Literacy helps students make responsible financial decisions.
    • Financial awareness builds Confidence, Responsibility, and Self-Reliance.
    • Practical business education enables students to understand the value of Earning, Managing, and Creating Wealth ethically.
    • Financially literate students are better prepared for their Personal, Professional, and Entrepreneurial journeys.

    Solution – Junior Business School

    Business Navachar School (BNS) offers a structured Junior Business School model that complements regular education by providing practical business learning, entrepreneurial thinking, and future-ready skills for BMC students.

    Junior Business School

    • A structured 4-Year Business Education Program for students of Std. 6 to Std. 9.
    • Designed to develop an Entrepreneurial Mindset from an early age.
    • Focused on Financial Literacy, Leadership, and Innovation.
    • Integrates Business Skills with regular academic education.
    • Builds Future-Ready, AI-Ready, and Industry-Ready students.
    • Supports the vision of NEP 2020, Startup India, and Viksit Bharat 2047.

    Weekly Business Education

    • 2 Days per Week dedicated to structured business learning.
    • 4 Hours of Weekly Learning through engaging activities.
    • More than 200 Learning Hours every year.
    • Progressive learning through a 4-Year Journey.
    • Consistent development of Business Knowledge, Life Skills, and Entrepreneurial Thinking.
    • Flexible model that complements students' Regular School Education.

    Practical Learning

    • Activity-Based Learning for better understanding and engagement.
    • Business Projects to apply classroom concepts.
    • Case Studies based on real business scenarios.
    • Role Plays to develop leadership and communication skills.
    • Business Visits for industry exposure.
    • Market Surveys to understand customer needs and business opportunities.
    • Guest Sessions by entrepreneurs and industry experts.
    • Startup Challenges to encourage innovation and creativity.
    • Business Exhibitions to showcase students' ideas and projects.
    • Focus on Learning by Doing, ensuring students gain practical experience rather than only theoretical knowledge.

    Teaching Methodology

    A practical weekly classroom structure that blends concepts, activities, projects, and reflection — the teaching methodology of Junior Business School.

    Every Week Structure (2 Hours)

    20 min Business Concept
    20 min Activity / Game
    20 min Case Study
    20 min Team Discussion
    20 min Project Work
    20 min Presentation & Reflection
    26

    Example – One Junior Business School Session

    A sample classroom session that shows how students Learn, Play, Practice, Present, and Improve — experiencing business instead of only reading about it.

    Topic Marketing & Selling
    Class Std. 7
    Duration 2 Hours
    1

    Step 1 – Learn

    20 Minutes

    The teacher explains:

    • What is Marketing?
    • What is Selling?
    • Why do people buy a product?
    • Simple examples of Amul, Parle-G, and Apple.

    Outcome: Students understand the basic business concept.

    2

    Step 2 – Activity

    20 Minutes

    Sell a Pen Challenge

    Each student gets one Pen. Students try to sell the pen to their classmates. They think about:

    • Who is the customer?
    • What should I say?
    • Why should the customer buy?

    Outcome: Better Confidence, Communication, and Sales Skills.

    3

    Step 3 – Business Game

    20 Minutes

    Students work in 4 Teams. Each team gets one product. Example:

    • Water Bottle
    • Notebook
    • Chocolate
    • School Bag

    Each team decides Product Name, Price, Offer, and Advertisement — then they present their product.

    Outcome: Learn Marketing, Teamwork, and Creativity.

    4

    Step 4 – Case Study

    20 Minutes

    Teacher shows a simple story of Parle-G. Students discuss:

    • Why is Parle-G popular?
    • Why do people trust this brand?
    • What makes it successful?

    Outcome: Learn from a Real Business Example.

    5

    Step 5 – Role Play

    20 Minutes

    Students act as:

    • Shopkeeper
    • Customer
    • Salesperson
    • Store Manager

    They create a small shop inside the classroom.

    Outcome: Improve Communication, Leadership, and Problem-Solving.

    6

    Step 6 – Presentation

    20 Minutes

    Each team presents:

    • What did we learn?
    • What was our business idea?
    • What can we improve?

    Teacher gives simple feedback.

    Outcome: Build Presentation Skills and Confidence.

    Home Activity

    Visit a nearby shop with your parents. Observe:

    • How does the shopkeeper talk to customers?
    • Which product sells the most?
    • What special offers are available?

    Share your observations in the next class.

    Final Learning Outcome

    After one session, students will improve their:

    • Business Thinking
    • Communication Skills
    • Confidence
    • Leadership
    • Sales Skills
    • Marketing Skills
    • Teamwork
    • Creativity
    • Presentation Skills
    • Problem-Solving Skills

    Our Learning Method

    Learn Play Practice Present Improve Lead

    Students don't just learn Business. They experience Business.
